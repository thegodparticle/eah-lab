Caitlin Dufour has lived in various places in Alabama and graduated with a B.A. in Psychology from the University of Alabama. She is pursuing her Doctor of Audiology degree at the University of South Alabama and has been a graduate research assistant in the Electric & Acoustic Hearing Lab since June 2026. Her interests include linguistics, particularly the study of prosody, and the interaction between cognitive psychology and hearing.
